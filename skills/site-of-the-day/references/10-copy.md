# 10 — Copy (Content = 10%, but it colours everything)

- "Defining a legacy in Formula 1, on and off the track." — Lando Norris (with his handwritten signature)
- "Speak their language" — MindMarket

Headlines from the *lowest*-scoring winners are **abstract adjective stacks**:
- "Thoughtful, intentional & scalable" · "Humanist Superintelligence" · "Where Intelligence Becomes Living" · "Projects that redefine spaces and the extraordinary" · "Rethinking spaces beyond expectations".

## Rules
1. **Say a thing only this subject could say.** Use its nouns, numbers and verbs (from the raw material inventory).
2. **Write the hero as a line, an instruction, or a name** — not a value proposition. ≤ 8 words for display type.
3. **One voice**: decide if it's dry, warm, deadpan, technical, playful — and keep it in microcopy (buttons, loaders, 404, cookie banner, alt text).
4. **Microcopy is design**: "Enter", "Sound on?", "Click to start", "Draw a zero", "Skip intro", "Back to the table". Label unusual interactions plainly.
5. **Metadata tells the truth**: dates, places, credits, counts, coordinates, version numbers. Real data reads as craft; invented data reads as template.
6. **Body copy**: 2–4 sentence paragraphs, specific claims, no filler adjectives ("innovative", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "elevate", "unlock", "empower", "next-gen", "world-class", "solutions").
7. **Never fabricate** clients, awards, testimonials, metrics, press. Use clearly marked placeholders if content is missing, and say so.
8. **Edges**: the 404, footer, loader and empty states get written too — winners use them as signature moments (Don't Board Me won SOTY with a 404).

## Quick filter
Delete any sentence that would still be true if you replaced the brand name with a competitor's.
